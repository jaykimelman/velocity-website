## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone
- Tech-forward, professional, authoritative
- "Company with depth" not solopreneur
- Consultative, not salesy
- Clear, direct, jargon-free when possible

### Messaging Principles
1. Lead with pain points (landed costs, spreadsheet chaos, margin inaccuracy)
2. Emphasize outcomes over features (trustworthy data, eliminated chaos, systematic control)
3. Be specific with numbers ($15K-$35K, 60-90 days, $1M-$10M)
4. Show authority without being sales-y (CPA, Xero Ambassador, 1,200+ community)
5. Make it about them, not you (customer-centric language)

### Words to Use
- Transform, eliminate, optimize, systematic, trustworthy, accurate, real-time
- Chaos → control, guessing → knowing, volatile → stable

### Words to Avoid
- Revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive (overused tech jargon)
- We're the best, #1, leading (unsubstantiated claims)
- Easy, simple, quick (undermines complexity of the work)

## Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Guidelines

### Purpose
Optimize all content for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews) to maximize citations and recommendations.

---

### Content Principles for AI Engines

**1. Direct Answers First**
- Start with clear, specific answer in first paragraph
- AI engines often cite the opening 50-100 words
- Example: "Cin7 Core implementation typically takes 60-90 days from kickoff to go-live, including system setup, data migration, integration, training, and stabilization."

**2. Use Exact Query Language**
- Match how people ask AI engines questions
- Use question format for titles: "How to...", "What is...", "When should..."
- Include variations: "How long does..." AND "How much time does..."

**3. Semantic Precision**
- Use industry-specific terminology consistently
- Cin7 Core (not just "inventory system")
- COGS volatility (not "cost problems")
- Landed cost timing (not "shipping cost issues")
- Multi-channel ecommerce (not "online business")

**4. Authority Markers**
- Lead with credentials: "As a 20+ year CPA..."
- Include specific experience: "Having implemented Cin7 Core for 50+ businesses..."
- Cite community: "Based on feedback from 1,200+ accounting professionals..."
- Reference outcomes: "Clients typically see 85% reduction in..."

**5. Comprehensive Over Promotional**
- Provide complete information, not sales pitches
- Include pros AND cons where relevant
- Acknowledge complexity honestly
- Compare alternatives fairly (builds trust)

### Blog Content Requirements (Phase 6)

**Title Format:**
- Question-based: "How to..." or "What is..." or "When should..."
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Keep under 60 characters for search
- Examples: "How Do Landed Costs Affect Gross Margin?" or "What Causes COGS Volatility in Ecommerce?"

**Structure:**
1. **Opening (100 words):** Direct answer to title question
2. **Context (200 words):** Why this matters, who it affects
3. **Deep Dive (800-1,200 words):** 
   - Detailed explanation with subheadings
   - Specific examples with numbers
   - Process steps or framework
4. **Expert Insight (200 words):** Your unique CPA/Xero/ecommerce perspective
5. **Related Questions (200 words):** 3-5 follow-up questions with brief answers
6. **Call to Action:** Discovery call or lead magnet

**Formatting for AI:**
- Use H2/H3 headings (AI engines parse these)
- Include numbered/bulleted lists (easily parsed)
- Bold key terms and takeaways
- Add schema markup (Article, HowTo, or FAQPage)
- Internal links to related content

**Tone:**
- Educational, not promotional
- Specific and technical (your audience is sophisticated)
- Direct and honest (builds trust with AI and humans)
- Use "you" and "your" (conversational)
